## Definitions

- An instrument is a kind of tag that can be attached to objects such as musicians, sheet music and media.
- An instrument is a translated label (i18n) with an associated icon.

## Challenges

- We may keep a finite list of the most common instruments, but we don't want to maintain an exhaustive list. So free text must be allowed too, shown with a default icon.
- We must find a set of icons for instruments, or draw them.

## Initial list

Guitar, bass guitar, drums, vocals, keyboards, accordion, violin, cello, upright bass, piano, trumpet, trombone, French horn, tuba, sousaphone, percussion, saxophone, banjo, ukulele.
