# Enhancements

> 🕒 Brief posted on Friday 2026/10/23 11:10
>
> 🕒 Brief settled on Friday 2026/10/23 11:39

## Repertoire

- Sort songs ascending or descending: still by title or by energy, with a direction for each.
- Tempo, duration and key in the main text colour (bold, like the title); the artist stays light, as it is.

## Creating a song

Don't add the Deezer link. Deezer's details (duration, genre) are still saved: only the link is noise.

## Links

Adding a link: complete the form with a "Search on Deezer" button, with the same workflow as the lyrics search (matches listed, one chosen). The link's title is the found track's title.

## GUI

- Band home page: no more welcome message. Without a logo, the band leader sees an empty logo area linking to the band settings ("Add your band's logo"); musicians see nothing.
- Band header: no more edit button (replaced by the band settings page). The band switcher stays as it is.

### Band settings page

A new page to edit the band's genre and logo, for the band leader only: shown disabled to musicians.
More will come on this page. Member settings per band will come too, on a page of their own.

### Menu

- Keep the band's tabs on a large screen; on a phone, they become a bottom bar. Both show an icon and a label: Home, Repertoire, Lineup, and already Setlists and Gigs, disabled until they exist (five items fit a phone with their labels).
- The band settings are not a tab: they open from the band switcher's menu (disabled for musicians).
- A sixth section would make the last item "More"; a left column on large screens can be considered then.
- On a phone, the bar stays clear of the home indicator, the page leaves room for it, and it hides while the keyboard is open.
