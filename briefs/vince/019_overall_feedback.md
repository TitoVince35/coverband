# Overall feedback

> 🕒 Brief posted on Wednesday 2026/10/21 18:24
>
> 🕒 Brief settled on Wednesday 2026/10/21 18:39

A list of desired enhancements.

## Instruments

Missing: turntable, MPC / sampler (or whatever they use in electronic music)

## Song creation

The first form should only ask for Title and Artist.
Once validated the user should land on the Song page.
The "details" accordion should be closed at first.

## Band logo

The band logo (small) should be next to the name of the band, on the common header.
And it should show also on then band's homepage, above the "Welcome..." but as large as possible with height 1/3 of a phone screen max. Centered, no background, no Card.
Also remove the background color parameter in the logo settings form.

## Loading animation

I want to see skeletons or spinners when data is loading. Sometimes a creepy blank screen happens. Not nice.

## Autofill project

Moved to its own brief: [020_autofill](020_autofill.md).

## Decisions

- Instruments: add turntables, sampler / MPC, synthesizer and drum machine (for now).
- Band logo in the header: the small logo next to the band's name; nothing when the band has no logo.
- No background setting: no background anywhere (option a). The band leader uploads a logo that reads on a dark background; the form's hint says so. The background column is dropped in a later release.
