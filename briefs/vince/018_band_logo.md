# Band logo

> 🕒 Brief posted on Tuesday 2026/10/20 20:46
>
> 🕒 Brief settled on Tuesday 2026/10/20 20:53

A band has a logo.
It may be uploaded by the band leader, from the lineup page.
The logo will be used on printed setlists so we need a good resolution.
A logo can be, round, square, or horizontal.
The logo will be displayed:
- On the band homepage
- As a miniature, on the band list.
